Our Story

About MUZIC MANTRA<

A family of passionate musicians dedicated to preserving Indian culture, empowering artists, and sharing the soul of Indian music with the world.

The Story

MUZIC Mantra was founded by Er. Deepak Kaushik during his student years with a vision of creating opportunities for talented musicians and preserving India's rich musical heritage. What began as a small group of friends has grown into a vibrant musical family, bringing together artists from across the country.

Today, MUZIC Mantra performs on national and international stages, sharing the beauty of Indian music with audiences around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote Indian music while empowering young artists through meaningful performance opportunities. Beyond entertainment, we use music to support social awareness campaigns, celebrate cultural traditions, and inspire positive change. Every performance reflects our belief that music is a bridge connecting people, cultures, and generations.

Musical Style

MUZIC Mantra blends devotional, folk, Bollywood, Sufi, classical, and regional music into unforgettable live performances. Whether performing at cultural festivals, television broadcasts, government events, or international concerts, our goal is to preserve tradition while presenting Indian music with a fresh and contemporary spirit.
